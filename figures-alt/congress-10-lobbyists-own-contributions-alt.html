Anthropic's network and Congress

Lobbyists' counted gifts rose across the displayed cycles; 2026 tilts Republican

What this is: gifts that registered lobbyists made from their own money, reported to Congress, for lobbyists at firms that have lobbied for a network client. Only gifts dated while the lobbyist's firm was registered for a network client are counted: $1.5M in all, more in each cycle shown, reaching $891K in 2026 so far, 59% of it to Republicans.
