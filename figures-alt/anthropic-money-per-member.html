Anthropic's network and Congress

Anthropic employees and its PAC gave $2.5M to 128 sitting members; 59% went to Democrats

What this is: campaign contributions to sitting members from people who list Anthropic as their employer, plus Anthropic's company PAC (ANTHROPAC), counted once each, to any of the member's committees (campaign, a campaign for another office, leadership PAC). $2.5M in all from 63 employees and the PAC: $1.5M to 77 Democrats (59%), $1.0M to 50 Republicans.
