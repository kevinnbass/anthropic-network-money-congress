Anthropic's network and Congress

Anthropic employees and its PAC gave $6.3M to 198 sitting members; 53% went to Democrats

What this is: campaign contributions to sitting members from people who list Anthropic as their employer, plus Anthropic's company PAC (ANTHROPAC), counted once each, to the committees the record ties to one member (campaign, a campaign for another office, leadership PAC, single-member joint fundraising committee). A gift that Anthropic's own registered lobbyist also reported as a lobbyist is counted with lobbyists' gifts, not here. $6.3M in all from 89 employees and the PAC: $3.3M to 111 Democrats (53%), $2.9M to 86 Republicans, $5K to one independent.

What the record does not show. An employee's gift is their own money, not the company's; the employer is as the donor wrote it on the filing. ANTHROPAC's money comes from its members and is given by the PAC. A contribution is not a vote. Pass-through and memo entries are counted once or not at all. Employees are counted by filed surname and first name, so a title, a middle initial or a hyphen does not make a second name, and a name filed from more than one city counts once; the count is of filed names, and two people who share a name would count as one. Anthropic's $40M in contributions to Public First Action went to that group, not to a member, and is not here (see figure 02). The latest contribution here is dated 30 September 2026; the 2026 cycle is still open. Members are the 533 serving on Nov. 26, 2026 (2 seats vacant); "sitting" means serving on that date.
