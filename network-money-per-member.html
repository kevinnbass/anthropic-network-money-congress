Anthropic's network and Congress

The network gave $2.2M to 185 sitting members in 2024–26, most of it from Anthropic staff

What this is: contributions to sitting members' own committees (campaign, a campaign for another office, leadership PAC) from the network's principals, from people who list a network organisation as employer, and from ANTHROPAC and ARI PAC, each counted once: $1.5M to 107 Democrats (70%), $665K to 78 Republicans.
