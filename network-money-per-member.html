Anthropic's network and Congress

The network gave $8.5M to 267 sitting members in 2024–26, most of it from Anthropic staff

What this is: contributions to the committees the record ties to one sitting member (campaign, a campaign for another office, leadership PAC, single-member joint fundraising committee) from the network's principals, from people who list a network organisation as employer, and from ANTHROPAC and ARI PAC, each counted once: $5.3M to 143 Democrats (62%), $3.2M to 123 Republicans, $5K to one independent.

Members are the 533 serving on Nov. 26, 2026 (2 seats vacant); "sitting" means serving on that date.
