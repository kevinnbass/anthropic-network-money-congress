Anthropic's network and Congress

The network gave $4.1M to 214 sitting members in 2024–26, most of it from Anthropic staff

What this is: contributions to sitting members' own committees (campaign, a campaign for another office, leadership PAC, joint fundraising committee) from the network's principals, from people who list a network organisation as employer, and from ANTHROPAC and ARI PAC, each counted once: $2.9M to 121 Democrats (71%), $1.2M to 92 Republicans, $5K to one independent.
