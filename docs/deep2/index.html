Deeper · II · The outside money

76% of what 24 committees that took network money spent on sitting members, from all their donors, went against Republicans.

Widens “The bigger money” from one race to all these committees’ spending on members serving on Nov. 26, 2026: money from all their donors, not the network’s alone.

Source · Federal Election Commission outside-spending filings

The filings identify the committee that spent the money. They cannot trace an expenditure to a donor.

The member rankings count spending from all donors, starting on each committee’s first reported gift date from a named network donor. See Anthropic’s Series A announcement.

Two cases behind the totals

A nonprofit paid a committee that opposed Bird, and personal donors gave to one that opposed Tillis. The cases span the 2026 and 2020 elections. The personal donors are on today’s network roster. The filings give no reason for the spending.
