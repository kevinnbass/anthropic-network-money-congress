What the record does not show. A bill's direction is a code for the bill, not a score of the sponsor. A lobbying report naming a bill says the client lobbied on it, not which way or whether the sponsor's office was contacted. A fellow is paid by the programme; the fellow and the office choose each other. A report counts for a member when it names a verified stand-alone bill the member sponsored: the number and the title must agree in the Congress of the report's year, or the filing must identify a bill of the previous Congress. Annual spending and authorisation bills cited for their provisions are not counted. For a client with several lines of work, the report must name the bill on the line the network funds. Cosponsor counts cover AI bills in the companion study only. Members are the 533 serving on Nov. 26, 2026 (2 seats vacant); "sitting" means serving on that date.
