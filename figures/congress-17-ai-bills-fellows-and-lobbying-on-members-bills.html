What the record does not show. A bill's direction is a code for the bill, not a score of the sponsor. A lobbying report naming a bill says the client lobbied on it, not which way or whether the sponsor's office was contacted. A fellow is paid by the programme; the fellow and the office choose each other. A report counts for a member only where it names, by number, a stand-alone bill the member sponsored, read in the Congress of the report's year; annual spending and authorisation bills cited for their provisions are not counted, and for a client with several lines of work the bill must be named on the line the network funds. Cosponsor counts cover AI bills in the companion study only. Members are the 533 serving on Nov. 26, 2026 (2 seats vacant); "sitting" means serving on that date.
