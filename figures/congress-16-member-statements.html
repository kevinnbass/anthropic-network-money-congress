Sources: hearing transcripts, committee reports, the Congressional Record, bill text, members' press releases and posts on X. Each statement is coded by what it does (names it, warns, calls for action and so on), not by whether it helps the company.

What the record does not show. A statement can name an organisation in praise, criticism or warning, and each counts once: the count measures mentions, not a relationship. Transcripts were searched by name, so a member who never uses the names is absent, not silent. Posts on X were collected for some accounts only, and 2026 is over-represented. Reports and bill text are counted under the venue where they are hosted. Statements naming private individuals are left out. Members are the 533 serving on Nov. 26, 2026 (2 seats vacant); "sitting" means serving on that date.
