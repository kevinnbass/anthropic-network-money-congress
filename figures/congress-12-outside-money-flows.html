Where the principals' outside money went

Sources: FEC contribution records filed by the receiving committees; BEMC 4 Association's 2022 IRS filing listing grants to four political nonprofits; and Anthropic's own announcements of its $40.0M to Public First Action, which does not disclose donors. Other individual givers are grouped into one line.

What the record does not show. A contribution is not an instruction: the committee spends as it chooses. Gifts to nonprofits that do not disclose donors do not appear, so Public First Action's funding beyond Anthropic's gift is a floor. FF PAC, SMP, LCV Victory Fund and MoveOn have many donors; a ribbon is what the record holds going in, not the committee's size. For the Public First committees the ribbons also carry their other itemised receipts and the transfers between them. Gifts filed under Dustin Moskovitz's trust ($150K) are drawn under his name. Refunds are not netted. Members are the 533 serving on Nov. 26, 2026 (2 seats vacant); "sitting" means serving on that date.
