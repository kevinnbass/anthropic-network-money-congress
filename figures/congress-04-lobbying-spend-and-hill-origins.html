Anthropic's network and Congress

Lobbying spend and the lobbyists' Hill origins

Sources: federal lobbying registrations and quarterly reports (forms LD-1 and LD-2) filed for network clients, 2016–2026. Spend is the amount printed on each quarterly report; the lower panel counts registrations and reports that name a lobbyist's prior government role. Variant client spellings are merged. Where a client's own in-house report prints an expense amount for a quarter, the figure uses that amount and leaves out what its outside firms reported as income from it for the same quarter.

What the record does not show. Reported amounts are rounded by the filer. A client's own in-house report already includes what it paid outside firms, so the two are not added; Tower 19's fees count as Aquia Group's subcontract for Anthropic, whose own report covers them (Tower 19's registration names Anthropic as the principal; audit X54). Reports before 2016 (RAND's and FAS's among them) are left out. A former government job is a job history, not evidence it was used. Amended reports appear once. Members are the 533 serving on Nov. 26, 2026 (2 seats vacant); "sitting" means serving on that date.
