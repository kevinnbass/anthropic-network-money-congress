Anthropic's network and Congress

Lobbying spend and the lobbyists' Hill origins

Sources: quarterly Senate lobbying reports filed for network clients, 2016–2026. Spend is the amount printed on each report; the lower panel counts only reports that actually name a lobbyist's prior government role. Variant client spellings are merged (Anthropic's own reports and those its outside lobbying firms file for it are one line); in a quarter with the client's own in-house report only that report counts, because it already includes the firms' fees.

What the record does not show. Reported amounts are rounded by the filer. A client's own in-house report already includes what it paid outside firms, so the two are not added; Tower 19's fees count as Aquia Group's subcontract for Anthropic, whose own report covers them (Tower 19's registration names Anthropic as the principal; audit X54). Reports before 2016 (RAND's and FAS's among them) are left out. A former government job is a job history, not evidence it was used. Amended reports appear once.
