What this is: each record is one filed document or one line in it: a super PAC ad buy, a campaign contribution, a campaign's payment to an AI company, a lobbyist's own gift, a grant, a federal award, a public statement. It shows when the paper trail exists, not how much money moved.

What the record does not show. Counts follow filing calendars and how deeply each period was searched: 2024–2026 were searched more thoroughly than 2016–2019, and one super PAC can file thousands of small ad-buy lines in a year. Records dated only by year (annual holdings, jobs with only a start year) are not in the bars. Most key moments are dated events copied from the timeline of a companion study, not rows reviewed in this pack.
