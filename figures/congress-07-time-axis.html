Election years spike with outside spending; 2025–26 shift to donations and AI-lab payments

What this is: each bar counts the dated, confirmed rows of the study's tables for that year: filings and lines in them (a super PAC ad buy, a campaign contribution, a campaign's payment to an AI company, a lobbyist's own gift, a lobbying report, a grant, a federal award), staff and fellowship records, trips and events, and public statements. It shows when the paper trail exists, not how much money moved.

What the record does not show. Counts follow filing calendars and how far each period was searched, and one super PAC can file thousands of separate lines in a year. Among campaign contributions the bars count every confirmed line a committee filed, including memo, redesignation, refund and conduit lines that no money figure adds. Records dated only by year (annual holdings, jobs with only a start year) are not in the bars. Most key moments are dated events copied from the timeline of a companion study, not rows reviewed in this pack.
