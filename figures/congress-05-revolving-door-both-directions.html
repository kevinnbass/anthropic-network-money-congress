Anthropic's network and Congress

The revolving door, both directions

Sources: House and Senate staff payroll reports, TechCongress and Horizon fellowship pages, lobbyists' disclosed former posts and organisations' staff pages. Each person counts once per direction, however many jobs they held.

What the record does not show. Taking a job is not evidence about the work done in it. Fellows count in the office that hosted them; who paid them is stated only where a payroll record was found. TechCongress is held as context under the roster criteria, so its placements are not counted as network-to-Hill moves. People for whom only one side of the move could be established are not drawn. Some staff directories could not be reached. Members are the 533 serving on Nov. 26, 2026 (2 seats vacant); "sitting" means serving on that date.
