Anthropic's network and Congress

The network reported 0.5% of 2025's AI-issue lobbying; the frontier labs 6%

What this is: every federal lobbying report (LD-2) from 2019 to 2025 whose specific-issue text names artificial intelligence or an AI bill by its AI title, from the Senate's lobbying records. Network: Anthropic and the network organisations that lobby. Frontier labs: Meta, Amazon, Google, Microsoft, Nvidia, OpenAI and xAI. Each amount is what the report states for all of its issues.

What the record does not show. A lobbying report's amount covers every issue on it, so these are upper bounds on AI lobbying, not estimates of it. A client's own in-house report already includes what it paid outside firms, so in a quarter with an in-house report for a documented common client the firms' fees are not added; amounts below the $5,000 reporting threshold are not counted, and expense methods may cover different activities. Some client identities and expense coverage remain unresolved, so these totals are provisional. Filings are matched to AI by their own issue text; a bill number alone was not enough, because many AI bills share numbers with unrelated bills in other sessions. Thirty filings the census held as adjacent algorithmic policy (other clients, none in the network) are left out. Lobbying is not a vote. Records through 2025.
