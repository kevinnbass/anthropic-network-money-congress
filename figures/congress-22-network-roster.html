What the record does not show. A record count shows how much was found, not importance: Jaan Tallinn's count comes mostly from a published grant list; a person whose giving runs through a group that does not disclose donors has a low count. Names are matched as described above, so a spelling the roster does not record is undercounted, and two people who share a first name and surname can be counted together. A tier is a documented relationship under written criteria, not a finding about conduct. Members are the 533 serving on Nov. 26, 2026 (2 seats vacant); "sitting" means serving on that date.
