Anthropic's network and Congress

Who the network is, and where each appears

Sources: the study's list of network organisations and people, matched to their FEC, lobbying, tax and congressional identifiers, and counted against every confirmed record by identifier or exact name. Tiers follow the study's written roster criteria (documented money, control, roles and a congressional function).

What the record does not show. A record count shows how much was found, not importance: Jaan Tallinn's count comes from a published grant list; a person whose giving runs through a group that does not disclose donors has a low count. Names are matched exactly, so unusual spellings are undercounted. A tier is a documented relationship under written criteria, not a finding about conduct; namesakes found by employer and city are left out.
