Anthropic's network and Congress

Which members the most kinds of connection touch

Sources: every confirmed record in this study linked to a sitting member. Money into super PACs and financial-disclosure links are left out: the first goes to committees rather than members, and the second touches only two members (household income from the Carnegie Endowment, a network grantee).

What the record does not show. Breadth is not weight: every kind counts the same here, whatever its size. A contribution is not a vote; outside spending is not coordination. Travel is mostly Aspen Institute conferences; a network-funded group is named as a sponsor of the 2025 conference, and network organisations supplied speakers at the others. Statements can be critical as well as friendly. A blank cell means no counted record was found, not that none exists; for members seated after the Congress began, some kinds were not searched or only partly searched. Members are the 533 serving on Nov. 26, 2026 (2 seats vacant); "sitting" means serving on that date.
