What the record does not show. The client did not direct the lobbyist's personal gift, and most of these lobbyists also serve clients outside the network. A contribution is not a vote. A lobbyist who left the firm still appears under it for the period reported. Gifts dated before the firm took a network client, or after it stopped, are held but not drawn. Count B follows the filings, not each person's work: a registration lists lobbyists from its effective date, a quarterly report names who lobbied that quarter, and B runs to 90 days after the last quarter that names the lobbyist; the filings do not date when a person started or stopped working for the client. Some re-checks could not be completed and are left out.
