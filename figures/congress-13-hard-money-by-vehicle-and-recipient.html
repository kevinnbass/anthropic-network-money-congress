What the record does not show. A contribution is not a vote. An employee's giving is their own, not the employer's; the employer is as the giver wrote it. Gifts made through ActBlue or WinRed are counted once, from the recipient's report; where that report is not yet filed or does not itemise the gift, it is not counted. Gifts a committee reports twice are counted once. A gift that an organisation's own registered lobbyist also reported on a lobbyist's contribution report is counted with lobbyists' gifts (figure 10), not here. Gifts to super PACs also appear in the outside-money figures; here they only show the split by kind of committee. 2026 is still open, so every bar is a floor. Members are the 533 serving on Nov. 26, 2026 (2 seats vacant); "sitting" means serving on that date.
