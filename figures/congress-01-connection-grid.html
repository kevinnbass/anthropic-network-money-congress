Anthropic's network and Congress

454 of Congress's 533 members have a documented connection to Anthropic's network

The widest kinds are lobbyists' own gifts, direct contributions and PAC contributions. Lobbyists' own gifts and super PAC spending are indirect, and for 120 of the 454 members they are the only kinds on record. 79 members have none. Sources: FEC, lobbying, grant, spending and travel filings and public statements, each record checked against the original document. A member counts under a kind of connection if at least one confirmed record links them; duplicates and searches that found nothing are left out. Dollar amounts are added up only within one kind of connection, never across.

What the record does not show. A contribution is not a vote, and outside spending is not coordination with the candidate. Super PAC spending and lobbyists' own gifts are indirect: the committee or the lobbyist acted, not the network's principals. Super PAC totals are those committees' counted spending for or against sitting members, and most of that money did not come from the network. Staff moves, holdings, travel and statements carry no dollar amount. Some FEC records could not be retrieved and are not counted. Lobbyists' own contributions, stricter count (the lobbyist is also named on the network client's own lobbying filings): 304 members, 1,019 records, $1.3M. Kinds with no counted record for a sitting member are not drawn (network money into super PACs and nonprofits; network grants to think tanks and advocacy groups; federal money to the network). Built 2026-11-27. Members are the 533 serving on Nov. 26, 2026 (2 seats vacant); "sitting" means serving on that date.
