What the record does not show. Most trips are Aspen Institute Congressional Program conferences paid for by the foundations on the sponsor line; the programme takes no corporate money, and Anthropic, RAND and Coefficient Giving appear as speakers, not payers. Attending a briefing is not a transaction. Dollar values are not drawn because most filings give none. Staff trips are the office's, not the member's. Senate forms are scanned images, read by machine and then re-checked by hand.
