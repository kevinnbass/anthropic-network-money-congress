What the record does not show. A grant to an organisation is not money to a member; no grant in the record reaches a member's office directly (fellowship placements are in figure 24). The largest grantees are research groups; the chart does not measure whether their work reaches Congress. Each grant is placed in one cause by what it paid for, from its stated purpose, its grant page or the recipient's own mission. Grants approved for future years are left out. Where a funder's tax return lists a payment that may be the same money as a published award drawn here, and no document settles it, the award is drawn and the payment is not added: 1 such payment, $3.0M. Grant lists lag and tax filings arrive a year late, so 2025–2026 are floors. Open Philanthropy's grants appear under its new name, Coefficient Giving. Members are the 533 serving on Nov. 26, 2026 (2 seats vacant); "sitting" means serving on that date.
