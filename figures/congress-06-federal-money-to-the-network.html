Anthropic's network and Congress

Federal money to the network

Sources: federal award records from USAspending and bill text from govinfo. Award amounts are money committed, as printed on the award record; the bill passages are quoted from bill text on govinfo and the Congressional Record.

What the record does not show. An award to an organisation is not a payment to a person, and money committed is not money spent. A bill that names an organisation does not mean the organisation asked for it. Some searches timed out and are gaps, not zeros. Look-alike names (such as a biotech firm with a similar name) are left out. RAND, CNAS, the Carnegie Endowment and FAS count only for awards on the lines the network funds there, from the date it first funded each line; their other federal work is held as context. Members are the 533 serving on Nov. 26, 2026 (2 seats vacant); "sitting" means serving on that date.
