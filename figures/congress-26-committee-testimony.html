Anthropic's network and Congress

Network witnesses before Congress: 58 appearances since 2015

What this is: every congressional committee hearing where the official witness list names the witness in a network organisation's role, from congress.gov, House committee pages and Senate hearing transcripts. For RAND, CNAS, the Carnegie Endowment and FAS only testimony on the work the network funds (AI, technology and security, biosecurity) counts; each dot is one appearance.

What the record does not show. Testifying is not lobbying, and a witness is invited by the committee. People who testified before joining a network organisation, or in a government role, are not counted. Senate witness lists come from hearing transcripts, which are published months late, so recent Senate hearings may be missing. An organisation counts only from the date the network first funded that line of its work; earlier appearances are drawn hollow and not counted. Eric Schmidt appears under the Special Competitive Studies Project and Schmidt Futures, organisations he and Wendy Schmidt fund and control.
